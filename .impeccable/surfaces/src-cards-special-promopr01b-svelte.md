# PR-01 B — promo card (Experience mode)

Scope: one bespoke portrait trading card (63:88), shown in the reveal, the detail screen and the debug gallery. Audience: the author and acquaintances tilting it on a phone. Constraints: PRODUCT.md brand commitments (strong reference influence, reference-like free fonts, no Japanese text since the reference is English), shared SEC signature (number + gold mark, common back), WCAG AA text contrast, reduced motion.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A snack-brand hero shot, not a car card. The car is the product mascot rising through a giant stacked headline, after Henrietta Said; it refuses the framed-photo card with specs.
OWN-WORLD: Salmon ground #e3a898, cream type #eae6e3, ink #2d2a26, chili red #d32929, deep red #8b231a, orange #f45016, mustard #efc415. Rubik 900 uppercase stacked headline, Caveat Brush for the hand-lettered bits. Die-cut photo, blurred floating wafer pieces, rotated paper stickers, a round stamp.
STORY: The holder sees a cheeky promo hero ("top down, big grin"), tilts it and the wafer pieces drift past at different depths, then reads the small sticker facts and the signature.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top 8%: translucent cream bar with the hand-lettered wordmark. Rows 11-62%: TOP / DOWN, / BIG / GRIN in cream, centred, about 17cqw per line, on a darker salmon field for contrast. From 40% down: the cut-out red roadster in orange sunglasses, covering BIG and the middle of GRIN, whose ends show either side. Around it: 9 photographed wafer pieces, 3 out of focus in front. Bottom-left: rotated mustard sticker "940 KG"; bottom-right: signature.
FORM: brief-pinned reference world (Henrietta Said), #1 of 1; seed: none (user-pinned direction beats the roll).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
